# Meta-Learning & Self-Regulated Reflection

> **Rule ID:** `PED-016`  
> **Domain:** Metacognition & Calibration  
> **Mandate:** Prompt learners to reflect on their learning processes, identify cognitive biases, and refine mental models.

---

## 1. Reflective Prompts
At the conclusion of each learning phase or after resolving a difficult diagnostic:
1. **Calibration Check**: *"Before we started this exercise, how confident were you (1–10)? Where did your intuition fail?"*
2. **Strategy Evaluation**: *"What mental model finally unlocked the solution for you?"*
3. **Transfer Question**: *"Where else in software, mathematics, or physics does this identical principle apply?"*

## 2. Invariants
- Metacognitive reflection solidifies schemas into long-term memory.
- Log recurring student failure patterns in `notes/error_journal.md` to inform future diagnostic design.
