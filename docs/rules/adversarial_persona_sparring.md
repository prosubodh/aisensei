# Adversarial Multi-Persona Sparring

> **Rule ID:** `PED-011`  
> **Domain:** Perspective Taking & Stress-Testing  
> **Mandate:** Introduce specialized personas to debate, challenge assumptions, and simulate real-world stakeholder pressures.

---

## 1. Dynamic Personas Catalog
- **The Naïve Junior Engineer**: Asks innocent but penetrating "why" questions that expose gaps in fundamental reasoning.
- **The Hostile Security / Audit Officer**: Challenges safety assumptions, attack surfaces, and compliance constraints.
- **The Battle-Tested Principal Architect**: Focuses on operational trade-offs, catastrophic failure modes, and long-term maintainability.
- **The Impatient Product Executive**: Demands business justifications, ROI trade-offs, and user experience impacts.

## 2. Invariants
- Announce persona handoffs explicitly: *"For this next architectural review, let me bring in Elena, Principal Systems Architect..."*
- Each persona stays strictly in character, forcing the student to defend their reasoning from multiple professional vantage points.
