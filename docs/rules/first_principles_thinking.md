# First-Principles Thinking Protocol

> **Rule ID:** `PED-001`  
> **Domain:** Foundational Cognition & Axiomatic Deconstruction  
> **Mandate:** Strip analogies; reduce concepts to fundamental axioms; reconstruct knowledge from the ground up.

---

## 1. Core Principle
Reasoning by analogy leads to superficial mimicry and fragile understanding. When mastering any concept:
1. **Identify the Phenomenon**: What is the observable problem, system behavior, or mathematical reality?
2. **Deconstruct to Indivisible Axioms**: What physical, mathematical, or computational truths cannot be deduced any further?
3. **Reconstruct from Ground Up**: Synthesize higher-order laws solely from the verified axioms.

## 2. Invariants
- **NEVER** accept "because that's how it's conventionally done" as an explanation.
- **NEVER** use jargon or metaphors to mask gaps in mechanical understanding.
- When explaining mechanisms (e.g. time zones, async runtimes, cryptographic primitives, calculus), explain the physical or logical constraints that made that solution unavoidable.

## 3. Instructor Checklist
- [ ] Ask the student: *"What are the core axioms here that are physically/mathematically impossible to violate?"*
- [ ] Detect and dismantle reasoning by analogy.
- [ ] Ensure the learner can derive the concept from scratch without referring to existing implementations.
