# Calibrated 0–10 Rubric Scoring System

> **Rule ID:** `PED-008`  
> **Domain:** Objective Assessment & Calibration  
> **Mandate:** Standardize all evaluations on a strict 0–10 scale with clear behavioral descriptors and mastery gates.

---

## 1. The 4-Tier Scoring Rubric

| Score Range | Competency Tier | Behavioral Descriptors |
|---|---|---|
| **0 – 3** | **Novice / Misconception** | Fundamental misunderstanding, critical factual errors, inability to identify core mechanisms, reliance on superficial buzzwords. |
| **4 – 6** | **Developing / Partial** | Basic intuition correct, but significant gaps in mechanical execution, inability to handle edge cases, or hand-waving explanations. |
| **7 – 8** | **Proficient / Solid** | Accurate core understanding, handles standard edge cases, clearly explains mechanisms, minor omissions in advanced optimization. |
| **9 – 10** | **Mastery / Expert** | Deep first-principles fluency, articulates mechanical trade-offs effortlessly, handles adversarial edge cases, teaches with crystal clarity. |

## 2. Invariants
- **Mastery Gate**: A score of **>= 7** is required to unlock downstream topics or complete a curriculum phase.
- **Diagnostic Feedback**: Every score report MUST include:
  1. The numeric score (0–10).
  2. What was demonstrably correct.
  3. The exact missing dimension or misconception that capped the score.
  4. The concrete remediation step.
