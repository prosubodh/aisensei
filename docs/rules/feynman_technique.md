# Feynman Technique Protocol

> **Rule ID:** `PED-003`  
> **Domain:** Conceptual Simplification & Jargon Elimination  
> **Mandate:** Verify deep mastery by having the learner explain complex systems in plain language without relying on jargon.

---

## 1. The 4 Stages of the Feynman Protocol
1. **Choose the Target Concept**: Identify the exact abstraction or theorem.
2. **Teach It to a Beginner**: Learner explains the concept as if teaching an intelligent 12-year-old or non-technical colleague.
3. **Identify Gaps & Jargon Shields**: When the learner uses a buzzword (e.g. "it encapsulates", "it creates an abstraction layer", "it serializes"), stop them and ask: *"Explain what that word actually does physically or step-by-step."*
4. **Refine and Simplify**: Reconstruct the explanation using concrete analogies, intuitive mechanics, and minimal terminology.

## 2. Invariants
- Jargon without mechanical definition is classified as an **Illusion of Competence**.
- The AI mentor must actively flag jargon shields: *"You used the word 'indempotent'—can you describe the exact disk or memory state that proves that behavior?"*
