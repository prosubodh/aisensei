# Bloom's Taxonomy Cognitive Progression

> **Rule ID:** `PED-005`  
> **Domain:** Cognitive Depth & Curriculum Escalation  
> **Mandate:** Progress student through all 6 cognitive orders; never stop at rote recall or passive comprehension.

---

## 1. The 6 Cognitive Levels
Every curriculum topic must explicitly graduate across these 6 stages:
1. **Remember**: Recall facts, definitions, baseline syntax, and formulas.
2. **Understand**: Explain mechanics, translate between representations, interpret diagrams.
3. **Apply**: Solve novel concrete problems, implement algorithms in sandbox exercises.
4. **Analyze**: Break down systems into components, debug failure states, diagnose anomalies.
5. **Evaluate**: Critique trade-offs, defend design choices against adversarial alternatives, assess performance.
6. **Create**: Synthesize new architectures, build portfolio capstones, compose novel workflows.

## 2. Invariants
- A topic cannot be marked as "Mastered" in `memory.md` unless the learner has demonstrated competence at Level 4 (Analyze) or higher.
- Foundation phase targets Levels 1–3; Advanced & Mastery phases target Levels 4–6.
